Endless Crossword

Daily mini crosswords

A small crossword you can finish in a few minutes, and then another, and another. Great for a coffee break, a commute, or a quick way to keep your English sharp.

Features

  • Thousands of 6×6 puzzles that get a little harder as you go
  • A new Daily puzzle every day, plus a calendar of past Dailies
  • Keep your streak going: solve at least one puzzle a day
  • Short, plain clues in everyday American English, written for this app
  • Type with a simple letter pad or a full keyboard
  • No ads, no accounts, no internet needed. Your progress stays on your device
